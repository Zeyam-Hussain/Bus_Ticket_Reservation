# Baggage and travel guidelines

Baggage: each passenger may carry one suitcase up to 20 kg in the luggage compartment and one small hand bag up to 5 kg on board. Extra baggage may be refused or charged by the staff.

Before travel: arrive at the boarding point at least 30 minutes before departure and carry your ticket and a valid CNIC or ID card. Departure times are shown on your ticket and booking confirmation email.

Not allowed: illegal items, weapons, flammable or hazardous goods.

The app does not currently show the boarding terminal. Please confirm the departure terminal with support before you travel.
