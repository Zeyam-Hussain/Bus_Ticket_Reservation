# Cancellation and refund policy

A booking can be cancelled before the bus departs. Cancelled seats are released for other passengers.

Refund rules: cancellations made at least 24 hours before departure are refunded in full. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before departure are refunded at 80 percent. No refund is given after departure.

Refund time: approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Refunds are reviewed and approved by the support team. The chat assistant can explain the policy but cannot approve, issue or promise a refund.
