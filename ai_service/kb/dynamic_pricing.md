# How fares and dynamic pricing work

AllRide uses dynamic pricing. A fare moves up or down from the route's base fare depending on expected demand, always within limits set by AllRide.

Factors that influence demand: how full the bus already is, how many hours are left before departure, the day of the week (Friday to Sunday departures are busier), and how many seats were booked in the last 24 hours. A busy bus close to departure usually costs more. An emptier bus far from departure usually costs less.

Predicted demand is described as low, moderate or high.

Fare lock: when you are logged in and view a bus, the fare is locked for you for 30 minutes. The fare you pay is the locked fare. If the lock expires you see a refreshed fare.

The fare is calculated by the booking system. The chat assistant only explains it and cannot change or negotiate a price.
