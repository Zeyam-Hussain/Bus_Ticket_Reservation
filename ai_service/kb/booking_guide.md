# How to book a ticket

1. On the home page choose the departure city, destination city, travel date and passenger gender, then search.
2. Pick a bus from the Available Buses list. Each card shows departure and arrival time, bus type, seats left and your fare.
3. On the seat selection page choose your seats in the 3D bus or the 2D view and pick Male or Female for each seat. Selected seats are held for you for 10 minutes.
4. Press Continue, choose a payment method (card, EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer) and press Pay Now. You must be logged in to book.

Seating rule: neighbouring seats must have the same gender unless both seats are booked by the same customer.
