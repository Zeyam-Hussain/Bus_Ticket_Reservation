# Tickets and booking status

After a successful payment your booking becomes Confirmed, you are taken to the home page where your ticket is ready, and a confirmation email with your booking ID, route, departure time, seat and amount is sent to your registered email address.

Booking statuses: Pending means the seat is reserved but payment is not completed. Confirmed means payment is completed. Cancelled means the booking was cancelled and the seat was released.

Reservation changes: a confirmed booking cannot be edited. To change the date, bus or seat, cancel the booking and make a new one.
