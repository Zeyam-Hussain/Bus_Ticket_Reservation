# Payments

Accepted payment methods are credit or debit card, EasyPaisa, JazzCash and bank transfer. Prices are in Pakistani rupees (PKR) with no service fee.

Payment statuses: Pending means the payment is not completed, Completed means it succeeded and the booking is confirmed, Failed means it did not go through and the booking stays Pending.

If a payment fails you can try booking again. The system does not store the reason a payment was rejected. Common causes are wrong card details, insufficient balance, bank limits or a network problem.

If money was deducted but you did not get a ticket, or you were charged twice, contact support immediately with your booking ID. This is checked by a human agent.
